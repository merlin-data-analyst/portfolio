## 📊 Featured Projects

### 1. Quick Service Restaurant (QSR) Operations Analysis

**Business Focus:** Operational efficiency and customer experience.

Analyzed restaurant operational data to understand waiting times, customer satisfaction, inventory availability, staff efficiency, and table utilization.

**Key areas explored:**
- Customer waiting time and satisfaction.
- Inventory availability and stock shortages.
- Staff efficiency and operational performance.
- Table utilization and menu performance.
- Business recommendations to improve restaurant operations.

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, MySQL, Power BI.

### 2. E-Commerce Sales Analysis

**Business Focus:** Sales performance and customer purchasing trends.

Explored e-commerce data to understand sales performance, product demand, order quantities, returns, and regional sales patterns.

**Key areas explored:**
- Total sales and order performance.
- Top-performing products.
- Product quantities and returns.
- Regional sales comparisons.
- Business insights to support sales decisions.

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, MySQL, Power BI, Google Sheets.

### 3. Website Background A/B Testing

**Business Focus:** Website conversion and user engagement.

Compared two website background variations to investigate whether background color was associated with differences in user conversion and time spent on the website.

**Key areas explored:**
- Comparison of conversion rates between two groups.
- Statistical significance testing.
- Analysis of time spent on the website.
- Interpretation of results to support website design decisions.

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Statistical Hypothesis Testing.

### 4. Supply Chain and Logistics Analysis

**Business Focus:** Delivery delays and logistics performance.

Explored logistics data to investigate delivery delays, operational risks, and patterns across different time periods and other available variables.

**Key areas explored:**
- Delivery delay patterns.
- High-, moderate-, and low-risk classifications.
- Time-based and operational comparisons.
- Correlation analysis and limitations of predictive modeling.
- Data-driven findings and opportunities for further investigation.

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, MySQL, Data Visualization, Machine Learning.

## 💡 My Approach to Data Analysis

My analytical process focuses on:

1. **Understand the Problem** — Identify the business question that needs to be answered.
2. **Explore the Data** — Examine the dataset, structure, and available variables.
3. **Clean the Data** — Handle missing values, inconsistent formats, and data quality issues.
4. **Analyze the Data** — Use appropriate queries, calculations, and statistical techniques.
5. **Visualize the Findings** — Present results through clear and meaningful visualizations.
6. **Communicate Insights** — Explain what the findings mean and how they may support business decisions.
